What we do not process: advertising or behavioural tracking data, precise location, contacts, payment details, ID numbers, biometric or health data. We do not use cookies for advertising, analytics or profiling. Gemma does not use automated decision-making or profiling.

Please note: Your username, "About" text, published games and their like counts are public. Do not put your real name, address, phone number or similar details in them.
